У сестер

Why “at the sisters’”?

In Ukrainian, “u sester” is how you say you’re going round to the sisters’ place — the kind of visit where the table is already laid and nobody leaves hungry.

That’s the whole idea: a small family restaurant with the dishes of a Ukrainian home kitchen. Borscht served the proper way, with salo, sour cream and a shot of vodka. Varenyky with potato or sour cherries. Deruny, cutlets and holubtsi.
